## Detailed Evaluation by Domain

### 1. Security & Cryptographic Integrity
- **Strengths:** SHA-256 evidence hashing, immutable audit ledger, hardware enclave support, biometrically enforced officer sessions.
- **Recommendations:** Implement post-quantum algorithm options (e.g. Dilithium) for 10-year forensic retention archives.

### 2. Multi-Carrier Telemetry Engine
- **Strengths:** Seamless real-time normalization of Safaricom, Airtel, and Telkom Kenya CDR & tower dump datasets.
- **Recommendations:** Expand direct API connectors for regional cross-border carriers across East Africa (e.g. MTN, Vodacom).

### 3. AI Threat & Pattern Synthesis
- **Strengths:** Gemini 2.5 server-side proxy prevents API key exposure; provides instant plain-language investigative summaries.
- **Recommendations:** Introduce localized Swahili & French fine-tuned prompt templates for regional field officers.

### 4. Commercial & Business Operations
- **Strengths:** Complete business readiness with real-time MRR/ARR metrics, partner portal, and interactive ROI estimator.
- **Recommendations:** Introduce self-serve enterprise tier upgrades via automated Stripe billing webhooks.
